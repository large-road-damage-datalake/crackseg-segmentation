# Abstract

CrackSeg Segmentation is a curated packaging of the CrackSeg dataset for pavement crack segmentation from on-board vehicle imagery.

The source data provides 3,540 high-resolution images (3840x2160) with pixel-level crack masks. Images were collected on roads in Shandong Province, China, and annotations were created in CVAT by a professional labeling team with additional verification. This package preserves publication metadata and adds generated statistics and visualization artifacts for the Large Road Damage DataLake.
